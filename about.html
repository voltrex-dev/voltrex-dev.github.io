Who we are

Green Oasis Lawn Care is owned and operated by Marshall. Marshall is an on-field operator, meaning he works on every job out in the field. Because of this, his availability is limited, and he can only take on so many customers at once.

This ensures that your yard receives the highest-quality service and care possible. There are no careless laborers damaging your property, leaving gates open, or doing poor-quality work. Marshall is the first person to arrive and the last person to leave your property during each visit.
